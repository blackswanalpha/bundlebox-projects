# 08 · Budget
- owes: two numbers that are never added, each labelled
- prevents: a saving that was invented

Two kinds of number appear and are never summed:

- **MEASURED** — read off a transcript after the fact: tokens used, cache reads.
- **ESTIMATE** — projected before the fact, printed as a range.

A model with no price is reported with tokens and no cost. A verb that could not
look says `unknown`. These are not hedges; they are what makes the other numbers
worth quoting.

**The wired total** is the equation in pattern 06, summed over lanes.

**The unwired counterfactual** — what the same build costs with no factory in
front of it — has four terms, and each one has to be named or it is a guess:

| term | why it is larger unwired |
|---|---|
| overhead | no lean flag stack: 44.3k against 29.2k per session, measured |
| orientation | the searches, listings and opened-then-discarded files that the brief replaced. Paid once per session, and there are more sessions |
| payload | whole files instead of located regions, still multiplied by churn |
| sessions | unpacked work does not fit; the same findings planned out to 5 sessions instead of 2 |

Report the difference as a **range**, labelled ESTIMATE, and show the terms.
A single number with no terms is a marketing claim.

**Write into the prompt:** the wired total, the unwired range, the ratio, and
the four terms with their sources.
